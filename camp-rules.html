Camp Rules & Guidelines

1. Respect & Teamwork

Be kind, respectful, and supportive to others. Teamwork makes the camp experience better for everyone.

2. Safety First

Follow all safety instructions from staff and leaders. Your safety and the safety of others is our top priority.

3. Active Participation

Be involved in all activities and give your best effort. Participation helps build confidence and great memories.

4. Care for Property

Take care of all camp property and the environment. Let's keep our camp clean and beautiful.

5. Positive Attitude

Come with a positive attitude and open heart. A great attitude makes the camp enjoyable for everyone!

Important Note

Failure to follow the rules may result in dismissal from the camp without a refund. Let's work together to create an amazing experience for all!
